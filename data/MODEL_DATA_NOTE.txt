ChronoFlux model data note
==========================

The bundled models were trained on data/training.csv: 47,678 cleaned, labelled
UNSW-NB15 flows. They are selected by the model arena (training/model_arena.py),
which records holdout metrics, feature importance and a label-ambiguity
(data-ceiling) analysis in models/current_stage/model_arena.json.

To retrain on a larger or updated benchmark:
1. Put a verified training CSV at data/training.csv.
2. Optionally put a testing CSV containing BOTH Normal and Attack classes at
   data/testing.csv.
3. Run:  python -m training.train_all

The forecasting world model learns from chronological sessions: the curated lab
progressions plus every live-sensor session recorded in data/live/. Recording real
attack sessions with live/sensor.py and retraining improves forecasting most.

Do not use an all-Normal testing file to claim attack-detection performance.
